How to use

  1. Choose a document, such as a Thai 1-inch ID photo or a passport.
  2. Take a straight-on photo with the guide, or pick one from your library.
  3. IDlens removes the background and sizes the photo. Pick a background colour, adjust the light, then tap Export.
  4. Save the 4×6 sheet, print it at 4×6 inches (10×15 cm) and cut along the lines.

FAQ

How do I get the best photo?

Stand in front of a plain wall in even light, look straight at the camera, remove sunglasses and hats, and keep your shoulders in the frame.

Where can I print?

Any photo shop, instant print kiosk or online print service. Order a 4×6 inch (10×15 cm) print without cropping or resizing — the file is already set to 300 dpi.

Why does it say "No face found"?

Retake the photo in better light, facing the camera, with only one person in the frame. You can also position the photo yourself by pinching and dragging.

Will my photo definitely be accepted?

IDlens follows published specifications, but acceptance is decided by the issuing authority. Check the latest requirements before you apply.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the app › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel the weekly plan?

Go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › IDlens Pro and cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Settings › IDlens › Language and choose Thai or English.

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
